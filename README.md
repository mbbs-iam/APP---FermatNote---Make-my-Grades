# Fermat-Note Project 🎓

> **Class Hub — Skillforge High School · Homework, Forum, Chat, and Notices for Students.**

**For class representatives and all motivated students who want to get involved for their class (limited spots)!**

Hi everyone!

Whether you plan to run for class representative or simply want to play a key role in your class this year, I've developed a project that will interest you!

This project is mainly aimed at class representatives, but also at all organized students who frequently use their phones and want to manage or enliven their class group. If you are not a class representative, this is the perfect opportunity to take on real responsibility, or even work as a duo with your class representative to lead this project together!

I am developing **Fermat-Note**, an application designed specifically for high school and higher education students to centralize everything: class chat, shared homework management, peer support forums, and collective organization. The app allows the class to be more organized and better connected in order to improve its performance.

* **A key and rewarding role:** Even without being a class representative, you become responsible for organizing the class group (in support of or in collaboration with the class representative).
* **A real bonus for Parcoursup:** Actively investing in class organization and peer support demonstrates concrete commitment (perfect for the "Civic Engagement" section).
* **A solid asset for your resume:** Excellent experience in project management, organization, and leadership, highly rewarding for summer jobs or your future career path.
* **Your classes will love it:** A free tool that simplifies school life and strengthens class cohesion.
* **Official recognition:** Participating classes and schools will be officially recognized among the pioneers of the project. You are part of a select minority of high school students who will be using it first!
